Leading Article

The position, plainly stated.

Software should grant dignity, not extract attention.

Most “AI” today is retention dressed as research. Keep the session, keep the profile, keep the person coming back. The alternative is simple: process the request, answer well, forget it. Run on the device when it can carry it. Ask before remembering, and prove you can forget.

Every product here is built that way. Slower to monetise, faster to trust.
