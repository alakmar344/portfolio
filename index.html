§IV · Editorial

Software should grant dignity, not extract attention.

— Al-Aqmar Tinwala

The positionMost “AI” today is retention dressed as research. Keep the session, keep the profile, keep the person coming back.

The alternative is simple: process the request, answer well, forget it. Run on the device when it can carry it. Ask before remembering, and prove you can forget.

Every product here is built that way. Slower to monetise, faster to trust.

I · Principle

Heart first

Built for human elevation — not doom-scroll retention or quiet surveillance.

II · Principle

Zero retention

No logging by default. Purge endpoints you can verify.

III · Principle

Uncompromising wonder

Useful and delightful are not a trade-off.

Yes, I’m thirteen. Yes, the systems work. ♡
