Field notes written by moonlight

Manifesto

Most AI today is retention dressed as research. Keep the session, keep the profile, keep the person coming back. The alternative is simple: process the request, answer well, forget it. Run on the device when it can carry it. Ask before remembering, and prove you can forget. Software should grant dignity, not extract attention.

Law I

Heart first

Built for human elevation — not doom-scroll retention or quiet surveillance.

Law II

Zero retention

No logging by default. Purge endpoints you can verify.

Law III

Uncompromising wonder

Useful and delightful are not a trade-off.
