ProfileThe Architect, v13

A thirteen-year-old is shipping AI that forgets you on purpose.

Eleven products are live. An LLM engine, a learning engine, a transit engine — all built on a single rule most of the industry has quietly abandoned: keep nothing.

By the desk of Al-Aqmar Tinwala Heart-First Intelligence

I didn’t start building AI for hype or a valuation. I started because the tools we hand to ordinary people should give them speed, privacy, and genuine cognitive lift — not a slower phone and a thinner sense of self.

So the work looks like this: adaptive EdTech that takes one question and turns it into three stages of real understanding. A privacy-first assistant with a 128k context window that never writes a user byte to disk. Molecular visualisations holding 60fps on a school Chromebook with no GPU to speak of.

Underneath it is a 2B-parameter transformer I wrote from raw tensor mathematics — attention, RoPE, RMSNorm, alignment — because you should be able to explain every layer of the thing you ship.

Heart first. If a system doesn’t treat the person using it with dignity, it doesn’t belong on the internet. That test has killed more of my features than any deadline.

§ IV · Editorial

Software should grant dignity, not extract attention.

— Al-Aqmar Tinwala, standing position

The positionMost of what gets called intelligence today is really retention: keep the session, keep the profile, keep the person coming back. That is a business model wearing a research paper as a coat.

The alternative isn’t complicated, it’s just less profitable. Process the request, answer it well, forget it. Run on the device when the device can carry it. Ask before you remember anything, and prove you can forget.

Every product on this page is built that way, and it is why some of them are slower to monetise and faster to trust. I would rather ship the second kind of software.

I · Principle

Heart first

Engineered for human elevation — not for doom-scroll retention or quiet surveillance. If it fails that test, it doesn’t ship.

II · Principle

Zero retention

Your queries, your identity, your half-formed thoughts stay yours. No logging by default, and purge endpoints you can verify.

III · Principle

Uncompromising wonder

Build for curiosity: atoms, motion, live transit, learning that sticks. Useful and delightful are not a trade-off.

Yes, I’m thirteen. Yes, the systems work. ♡
